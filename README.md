### Robert Spencer

AI Engineer & PhD Researcher at The University of Queensland (SAAFE CRC), based in Brisbane. I build applied machine learning, clinical LLM, and privacy-preserving simulation systems for health and environmental problems.

#### Current work
- **[VetNutriLM](https://github.com/robertjspencer/vetnutrilm):** Open-source, data-free QLoRA pipeline (Gemma 31B, Qwen 27B, Mistral 119B) for structured feeding-history and diet-category extraction from veterinary electronic health records (`>94%` binary F1, `>92%` macro F1 across 6 diet classes, rolled out over 38,500+ consultations on UQ's Bunya H100 cluster).
- **PhD in AI for Epidemiology (UQ / SAAFE CRC):** Privacy-preserving digital twins for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) risk assessment across One Health, spanning an 18,500+ record PRISMA-ScR scoping review, multi-year open-cohort livestock models, and federated and state-space surveillance benchmarks using differentially private synthetic data.
- **Commercial AI Engineering (Beyond Data):** Contract AI Engineer supporting technical scoping, data analysis, and AI/ML solution design.

#### Track record

- **Radix Nutrition (Senior Data Scientist):** First technical hire; built Python/PyTorch formulation-optimisation and FSANZ evidence systems cutting iteration time by `>90%`, formulation costs by `>25%`, and substantiating 150+ health claims.
